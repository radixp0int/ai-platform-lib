The rule the chat UI already follows, carried onto the canvas: severity picks the placement, not just a colour. Accent means a person has to act. Danger means something broke. A step that is merely waiting gets neither — it gets a dashed edge and a duration.

Nunito's rounded terminals read a step lighter than Libre Franklin at the same weight, so every font-semibold in the shell wants to go to font-bold, and font-bold to font-extrabold. Its tabular figures are good, so the duration stamps stay in a column.

Two declarations plus the Google Fonts link in index.html (400·500·600·700·800). Every colour, radius and shadow on this sheet is already a token in brand.css — the flow viewer introduces none of its own.
