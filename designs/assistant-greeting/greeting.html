3AFloating widget

Opens from a launcher in the corner. When it is closed, hover the launcher for start-from questions. The two demos show how the minimized launcher reports a finished task and a dropped connection.

The task demo starts a reconciliation and closes the widget. The error demo closes the widget while a reply streams, then drops the connection. Timings are sped up.
  • While it runsThe launcher shows a progress ring and the avatar keeps thinking.
  • When it’s doneThe ring turns green, the avatar hops with a happy squint and shows a check, then a dot stays until you open the widget.
  • Start-from on hoverHover the closed launcher to see the questions. They stay hidden while something is running or a result is waiting.
  • If the connection dropsThe partial reply stays and it retries three times. Minimized, the launcher shows an amber ring while it retries, then a red ! until you open it.
