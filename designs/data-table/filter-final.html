Data table filters, final look

One omnibox, one panel, two styles. Simple is the Filters button and panel, plus an omnibox that suggests dates and values as you type. Advanced keeps all of that and adds quick dates inside the omnibox: click it and pick a preset for any date, or a value for any filter. Every active filter is a chip inside the omnibox in both styles, and switching never loses a filter.

Table Filter style
  • Click the omnibox
  • Type renew, 30 or act
  • ⌘K or Ctrl K focuses it, ⌫ removes the last chip
  • Open Filters for the full panel
Ship first: Simple

The minimum, and already complete

  • Omnibox: chips wrap to a second line, and after the two search groups it lists matching Dates and Filters. Each date suggestion names its field.
  • Filter panel: a Dates section with one row per date, each with presets and a From/To range, then the status and plan toggles.
  • Chips: every filter is an omnibox chip, dates included, with a dimmed field name in front: started: Last 90 days.
Then: Advanced

One addition, and a setting

  • Omnibox: a quick slot, shown when the field is empty and focused, with a row of presets per date and a row of values per filter. The dropdown is wider (40rem) for it.
  • Date fields: each carries a direction, past or future, so presets read correctly: Due says Overdue and Next 7D, Created says 7D and 30D.
  • Setting: "Filter style" per person, on every table. The switch lives in the panel footer and the quick dropdown.
