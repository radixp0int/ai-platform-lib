How much should I be putting into savings each month?

Start with your monthly surplus: income after fixed costs and typical spending. Whatever that number is, automating a transfer on payday matters more than the exact amount 3, because consistency beats optimization at this stage 1.

A practical split many people use:

  1. Keep one month of expenses in checking as a buffer 1.
  2. Direct new savings to a high-yield account until you reach your target. Rates vary widely between providers 2, so it pays to compare.
  3. Only after that, route the overflow toward investing or extra debt payments 5.
